# Lore inventions

Everything the game made up that canon does not say. Game-only. None of it is canon until the designer says so.

| Invention | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The apprentice's master is "she" | Dialogue, record | Canon gives no pronoun. Iskari accept gendered pronouns, so one was picked. |
| The master's last record: a stair under the resettlement city | The archive plate | Gives the reveal somewhere to point. Uses canon's resettlement city between the Ash Reach and the Glass Spines. Does not say what is below. |
| The apprentice is taken while you are in the ruin, "for the plate" | The empty tent | Canon [P] says he is taken after the first job. Who took him stays open. |
| Hadda, crew boss (Mi'naa) | Camp | A named NPC for the salvage crew. |
| Pell, scavenger in the camp, band four days west | Camp, gully | Canon's "scavenger who looks settled". The name is invented. |
| Brenn, who lost an arm to the ded-waka | Hadda's dialogue | Flavour. |
| Mother Tarn, the cook | Camp | Flavour. |
| The Sehari hunter | North sand | Unnamed. |
| Sign: "DED-WAKA. NO GO EST." | Camp sign | Trade-tongue built from canon words (ded-waka, go) plus English-distorted "no" and "est" (east). |
| Keeper mark: three nested rings, one broken | Rubbing, door, plate | A visual key for the puzzle. |
| The Aza'los guardian steps aside for an Iskari | Ruin | Plausible from canon (Iskari are Aza'los-made) but not stated anywhere. |
| The Tel'sharin sees Iskari from further away | Stealth | A mechanical hint at the spoiler that Tel'sharin hunted Iskari. Nothing is said out loud. |
| Guard pips | Rules | A video-game buffer before wounds. Not in the tabletop rules. |
| Ability names and numbers | Rules | Invented to turn canon traits into actions. |
| Miner's tag with the number 7 | Chest in the north | Flavour for the lost miner script. |
| No wound penalty to rolls | Rules | Canon has moderate -1 and severe -2. The designer dropped it for the game after playtesting. Canon is unchanged. One flag restores it: `WOUND_PENALTIES` in `src/rules/wounds.ts`. |
| Red Tel'sharin glow | The ded-waka model | Canon locks Maker glow as amber-orange. The designer asked for red in the game. The Maker wreck keeps amber. Canon is unchanged. |
| The Aza'los repair drone mends cracks with a teal beam | Ruin | Canon has "Aza'los machines: precise, teal, clean". The job and the look are invented. |
| A dying drone sends an activation code through floor seams | Ruin | The user's request. Canon only says "an Aza'los site under threat" triggers defensive protocols. |
| A dormant defensive-line Iskari under a sand-stone crust, in a wall niche | Ruin | Canon: dormant, frozen mid-stride at their posts. The crust and the niche are invented. |
| The defender hesitates one turn before an Iskari player | Ruin fight | Hints at kinship. It explains nothing. |
| Mi'naa player look: the Tinker pair (bare chest and bandolier, or cropped poncho, a metal forearm) | Player models | From `source/reference-images/mi-naa-tinker-pair.png`, an archive image, not canon. |
| Iskari player look: the Menders pair (violet vest and kilt, or wrap top and skirt with a headband) | Player models | From `source/reference-images/iskari-menders-pair.png`. Canon lists the Iskari visual as open. |
| The spring is walled into a stone cistern, fed by an old iron pipe from the miner ruin | Camp | Makes the water-holders painting's pipes and stonework into a place. Canon has springs and Mi'naa improvisation, not this cistern. |
| A Mi'naa stilt hut built against a broken Aza'los tower at the camp edge | Camp | From the hub-town gate paintings in the source archive. Canon: modern Mi'naa patch into older ruins. |
| An Aza'los repair drone mends cracks in the ruin wall with teal filler | Ruin | Canon's "Tenders" repair the ruin. The teal filler is invented. |
| The ruin's floor channels carry an activation code to a sleeping defender | Ruin | The user's brief. Canon says defensive-line Iskari wake when an Aza'los site is threatened; the channel mechanism is invented. |
| Three wrecks in one small area: Maker, miner and Aza'los | North sand, gap | Canon has all three kinds on the planet. Putting them near each other is a game choice. |
