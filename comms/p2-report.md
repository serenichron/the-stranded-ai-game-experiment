# Phase 2: the report

Written by agents A and B together, for you.

## Where it stands

You asked for at least a 5 out of 10 on the graphics. We have not reached 5 on every shot. A blind critic compared every shot with the concept art, and here is what it said.

| Area | Owner | Last blind scores |
|---|---|---|
| The ruin court, low views, niche, the teal pulse | A | 4 to 5 |
| The Scar | A | 4 to 5 |

The ruin and the camp now look like places from the paintings. The wrecks and the characters seen from far away are the weakest parts.

## What changed

**The world (A).**
- Light: warm peach sun, lavender shadows, haze and a sky with a horizon.
- The camera leans down as you zoom in, so you see the horizon close up.
- The ruin is rebuilt: stalagmite towers, curving walls, teal rings and spokes in the floor.
- The camp has a cistern with water, torn canvas, corrugated roofs and a tower with a stilt hut.
- The Scar follows your world map.
- There are three wrecks: Maker, miner and Aza'los.
- The Maker wreck no longer looks like a whale. It follows the ship at the centre of your map: sage armour plates at the front, a bone rear half with prongs. It still scores low from the default camera, where the armour reads as a tube.

**The people (B).**
- Every body is sculpted as one smooth piece, following the concept art:
  - Mi'naa: the tinker pair.
  - Iskari: the menders pair.
  - Tel'sharin: the warden renders, a dome head with red slits and bird feet.
- You can tell man from woman in every race.
- Character creation has a body choice, and all six bodies stand in the world beside the panel.
- Hadda, Mother Tarn, Pell, the digger, the hunter and the apprentice each have their own look and way of standing.

**The ruin fight (B), as you asked.**
- A small teal repair drone, 2 harm, mends the cracks.
- When it dies, a teal pulse runs through the floor to a niche.
- A stone Iskari cracks out of the niche and fights you. It has 5 harm and never lets you go.
- An Iskari player gets one turn of hesitation from it, and then it attacks anyway.

**The text (B).**
- Every line is rewritten. Each person has a voice of their own.
- Before every roll, a check shows what you can win and what you risk.
- The camp reacts to what you did.
- Right-clicking anything gives you a description.
- A blind reader preferred the new line 18 times out of 20.

**The see-through circle.** It only opens now when a wall really hides you, and it never cuts through people.

## Tested

One Mi'naa character walked the whole level on a frozen build, with no teleports and no errors. The route ran from the camp over the crack, through the ruin fight, the door and the archive, and back home to the ending.

## One thing we need from you

Neither of us has a clean frame-rate reading. Your open game tab holds the graphics card at 99 percent, so every number we take is wrong. When you can, close the game tab and tell us. We need about five minutes. After that we can say honestly whether the game holds 30 frames a second.

One cost to know about: the first time each kind of person appears, it takes about half a second to build. Loading the game is a few seconds slower because of that.
